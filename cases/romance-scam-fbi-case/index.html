Questions & answers

What are the signs of a romance scam?

Three lines: "Let's move to WhatsApp", "I can't video call", and a request for money (customs, hospital, lawyer, "I'll pay you back Friday"). The FBI lists all three.

Should I send money to someone I met online?

No. The FBI: "Never send money to anyone you have only communicated with online or by phone."

He wants to use my bank account. Is that OK?

No. The FBI says someone who needs your account is most likely using it for other fraud, and you can end up charged yourself.

How do I check if a profile photo is fake?

Run a reverse image search (Google Lens, TinEye). The same face under other names, or on a stock photo site, means it's fake.

He never asks for money, just shows me his trading profits?

That's "pig butchering", the investment version of the romance scam. Crypto investment fraud cost Americans over $7.2 billion in 2025 (FBI IC3).

Someone says they're from the FBI and asks for money?

Hang up. The real FBI never asks for money, gift cards or crypto. Call 1-800-CALL-FBI yourself.

Already sent money?

Stop all contact, call your bank right away, and report: U.S. ic3.gov and reportfraud.ftc.gov · U.K. Report Fraud (reportfraud.police.uk) · Canada Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre · Australia Scamwatch and ReportCyber.

Who is Emuobosan Hall?

Sentenced to 8 years in a $3.5 million romance scam case; failed to report to prison on March 25, 2026. The FBI offers up to $5,000 for information: 1-800-CALL-FBI or tips.fbi.gov. She is presumed innocent of the new charge.
