事件

GTA 6はPS5とXbox Series X|S専用で発売され（Metacriticで99点）、詐欺師は準備万端でした。NordVPNはダークウェブで200ドルの詐欺キットを発見。中にはログイン中のセッションを盗む偽の「GTA6 Keygen.exe」が入っていました。Xでは少なくとも7つのアカウントが、数時間のうちに「AIでGTA 6がPCで動いた」という同じ文章を投稿しました。

覚えておきたいこと

  1. ゲーム史上最大のリークは、ホテルの一室とテレビ用スティック、そして17歳の少年から生まれました（BBC）。
  2. 本物のゲームがウイルス対策ソフトをオフにするよう求めることはありません。
  3. SMSで届いた認証コードはあなただけのもの。サポートにも友人にも教えないでください。

よくある質問

GTA 6のPC版はある？

ありません。PS5とXbox Series X|Sのみです。「GTA 6 PC版ダウンロード」はすべて偽物で、研究者はパスワードを盗むマルウェアを確認しています。

エミュレーター、AnyPS5、キージェネレーターは？

現時点では使えません。「GTA6 Keygen.exe」ファイルはマルウェアを仕込みます（NordVPN、2026年11月18日）。

無料のGTA 6コードやプレゼント企画は？

本物のプレゼント企画が、ログイン、手数料の支払い、QRコードの読み取りを求めることはありません。

フィッシングの波はどのくらい大きい？

KnowBe4：ゲーム関連のフィッシングメールが1,300%増加。10月31日〜11月1日には、同社が処理したフィッシングの42.4%がGTAを装っていました。当チャンネル創設者の推定：偽のGTA 6ファイルですでに1,000台以上のPCが感染。

ダウンロードやコードがうまくいかない？

PlayStation、Xbox、Rockstarの公式サポートだけを利用してください。コメント欄の「修正ファイル」を入れたり、PCにコマンドを貼り付けたりしないでください。

GTA 6のアプリやAPKは？

ありません。SMSの読み取りを求めるアプリは、銀行やメールの認証コードを盗むおそれがあります。

GTA 6をリークしたのは誰？

Arion Kurtaj（Lapsus$）、2022年9月のRockstarハッキング。2026年11月16日に有罪を認め、量刑言い渡しは11月23日。

「GTA 6」ファイルをもう実行してしまった？

安全な別の端末からパスワードを変更し（まずメールと銀行）、すべてのセッションからログアウトし、2段階認証をオンにしてから通報してください：米国 ic3.gov · 英国 Report Fraud · カナダ CAFC · オーストラリア ReportCyber。

0:00GTA 6 download: before you click

GTA 6 download? Before you click, STOP. Ninety stolen clips, hundred-gigabyte fakes, and your passwords. But how, and why do you never check?

The teenager who stole GTA 6 from Rockstar did it from a hotel room, while police were protecting him. Two days ago, in London, he pleaded guilty. And the game he leaked has become the perfect bait: fake copies that empty your accounts. Everyone's warning you about the hacker.

The real danger is one line in a file that asks you to do something. By the end, you'll know that line, and the seven traps waiting for every GTA fan this week.

0:34The hotel room: how GTA 6 was hacked

September, twenty twenty-two. A roadside hotel in Bicester, near Oxford. In one room, a seventeen-year-old named Arion Kurtaj. He's out on bail for earlier hacks, and he isn't hiding from the police.

The police are guarding him, from rival hackers who want the bitcoin he's accused of stealing. His computer has been taken away. So, according to the case, he plugs a TV streaming stick into the hotel television, adds a wireless keyboard and mouse, and goes back to work. He gets inside Rockstar's internal chat.

He takes ninety clips of unfinished GTA 6, almost an hour of gameplay, and he threatens to publish the game's source code unless Rockstar contacts him within twenty-four hours. Rockstar says no. So he posts everything on a forum, under the name TeaPot Uber Hacker. It becomes the biggest leak in video game history.

Rockstar later put the cost at five million dollars, and thousands of hours of staff time. And that name, Uber, was a clue.

1:25Lapsus$: the teenagers who hacked giants

Kurtaj was a leading member of a gang called Lapsus. Just days before Rockstar, they broke into Uber. Prosecutors say that attack alone cost Uber more than two point eight million dollars. They also hit the bank app Revolut, and Britain's biggest phone networks.

Their main weapon wasn't brilliant code. It was people. They tricked employees into letting them in. This is the playbook to remember, because it will be used on you.

Pretend to be someone with authority. Make it urgent. Keep asking until the tired person says yes. The same three tricks are waiting in every fake GTA download.

In twenty twenty-three, doctors found him unfit to stand trial because of severe autism. A jury only had to decide whether he did it. They decided he did. A judge sent him to a secure hospital, possibly for life.

Then, this January, a routine assessment found him fit. He was moved to an ordinary prison. And on Monday, three days before GTA 6 comes out, he pleaded guilty to everything. He'll be sentenced on November twenty-third.

Remember this: the biggest leak in gaming history came from a teenager, a hotel TV, and a few tricked employees. But his story didn't end in court. Because the moment those clips went online, a new kind of criminal saw something else: bait.

2:36His leak became bait: fake GTA 6 PC downloads

August, twenty twenty-six. An anonymous leaker calling itself Cyberleek starts posting new GTA 6 clips, and every clip comes with an ad for its own crypto coin. Fans can even vote with the coin on what leaks next. Then the money moves out.

Reports say around a quarter of a million dollars was withdrawn in a single day, and the coin crashed. At the same time, a "leaked full copy" of GTA 6 spreads on torrent sites. A hundred and thirteen gigabytes. Testers opened it.

Almost all of it was empty zeros, padding, to look like a real game. Hidden inside, a tiny piece of code, about fifty kilobytes. Its first job: switch off your antivirus. Five days ago, researchers at Cynet traced another one.

A link promising a GTA 6 download led to a fake software site, and then to a small zip file. Inside was a stealer: a program built to steal. Your saved passwords, your browser logins, your Steam and Roblox accounts, your crypto wallets. And here's the line I promised you.

The file came with instructions: run it as administrator, ignore the security warning, and if your antivirus blocks it, add an exception. No real game ever asks you to turn off your protection. That one line is the whole scam. Others don't bother with files.

Researchers at ESET found people selling a fake "developer build" for forty dollars, and "early access" for two hundred and fifty, paid in crypto. Here's the truth: GTA 6 comes out on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S. There is no PC version. There is no early access.

Anything that says otherwise is the trap.

4:00Launch day: the scammers were ready ($200 scam kit)

And now it's live. The day GTA 6 came out, first in New Zealand, critics gave it ninety-nine out of a hundred. Within hours, at least seven accounts on X posted almost the same words: an AI just got GTA 6 running on my PC. Same text, different accounts.

And NordVPN found a ready-made scam kit on the dark web, for two hundred dollars in crypto. Inside: a fake GTA 6 keygen that steals your logged-in sessions, a crypto wallet drainer, and a bot that pushes the links on YouTube Shorts. Their verdict: there is no PC version. If you see one, it's a scam.

And the phishing came first. Security firm KnowBe4 says gaming phishing emails jumped thirteen hundred percent. On one weekend before launch, forty-two percent of all the phishing it caught was pretending to be GTA. Pages with AI pictures of Lucia and Jason, a "four thousand dollar bonus", and a fake casino behind it.

The hackers didn't wait for the game. They were ready.

4:54The old game: GTA Online scams and the FBI

None of this is new. For more than ten years, GTA Online players have been hunted the same way. "Free" Shark Cards. Money generators that ask for your Rockstar login, then a "human verification".

There is no generator. The page exists to steal the account, along with any card saved on it. Then there are the shops selling "modded" accounts and cheat menus. The account gets banned, or the seller takes it back.

And Rockstar's owner goes to court. In one case in New York, a cheat maker was ordered to pay a hundred and fifty thousand dollars. But the most dangerous version came from a place gamers trust most. An official game store.

The FBI's Seattle office is now looking for victims of games on Steam that were laced with password-stealing malware, between May twenty twenty-four and January twenty twenty-six. One of them, PirateFi, sat in the store for about a week. Even the official shop isn't a guarantee. Which brings us to the people the FBI worries about most.

It's not a game. This could be happening to your child as you watch this video. I'm Bill Sweeney. I head up the FBI in New York City, and I'm asking parents and guardians to pay attention to this.

As soon as that instant message pops up on your child's browser or on their smartphone, there could be a sexual predator on the other end. It takes only a few days, sometimes just hours, to groom your child into sending compromising photos to the person on the other end of that chat. Then the threats begin. In online games, free money is often the first message from an adult who isn't there to play.

The FBI's advice to parents: know who your kids talk to inside the game, and make sure they know they can tell you anything. Now, the next wave. Because this year, the scammers have a new partner.

6:317 traps: phishing, keys, remote access, SMS stealers, AI voices

The FBI has already warned: criminals use artificial intelligence to write perfect messages, make fake photos and videos, and even clone the voice of someone you know. So here are seven traps for GTA week. Each one is built on a real case that already happened. Then, what it will look like this week.

Then, how to spot it in three seconds. Trap one: fake gameplay videos. Researchers at Check Point helped remove more than three thousand YouTube videos promising game cheats and free software, with malware in the link and fake comments saying "it works". Now picture an AI-made video of "GTA 6 on PC".

Trap two: the deepfake livestream. A fake stream with an AI copy of a tech company's boss drew around a hundred thousand viewers. Expect a "famous streamer" giving away GTA 6 codes. Real giveaways never ask you to log in, pay a fee, or scan a code.

Trap three: phishing pages. Fake shops already sell GTA 6 for crypto, with AI-made pictures. Some buyers say their boxed copy came without a code, so watch for pages offering to "restore your missing code" with a PlayStation or Xbox login. Log in only inside your console.

Trap four: remote access. Thousands are searching "GTA 6 download error" tonight. Fake support will offer to fix it, if you install their program, or paste a command into your computer. Real support never asks for either.

Trap five: cheap keys and accounts. A code for fifteen dollars that's already used. A "shared account" the seller takes back. An account with a billion dollars of game money that gets banned.

Buy only in the PlayStation Store, the Microsoft Store, or a real retailer. Trap six: apps that steal your text messages. Researchers at Zimperium found more than a hundred and seven thousand malicious apps built to read the security codes we get by text, from more than six hundred services, in a hundred and thirteen countries. Expect a "GTA 6 companion app".

There is no GTA 6 for phones. And an app that asks to read your texts is a thief. And trap seven: your friend's voice. The FBI warns AI can copy a voice from a short clip.

So a call from a "friend" on Discord: "I sent you a code by mistake, can you read it to me?" That code unlocks your account. A code that comes by text is for you only. Nobody else. Not support, not a friend.

8:40What to do tonight (and if you already clicked)

So, before you play tonight. One: GTA 6 comes only from your console's store. Two: no PC version, no early access, no phone app. Three: no game ever asks you to switch off your antivirus.

Four: turn on two-step login for PlayStation, Xbox, and Rockstar. Five: never share a code you get by text. Already ran a file? Change your passwords from a different, clean device, starting with email and bank, and log out of all sessions.

Then report it. In the U.S., to the FBI at IC-three dot gov. In the U.K., to ReportFraud. In Canada, the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre.

In Australia, ReportCyber. By my estimate, more than a thousand computers are already infected by fake GTA 6. There is no PC version. Any file like that is a virus.

Never tell anyone a code from a text. Show this to your son. The hacker will be sentenced on Monday. The fakes will keep coming for months.
