About Breach & Trace

Breach & Trace investigates real fraud and cybercrime cases from the U.S., Canada, the U.K. and Australia: romance scams, fake websites, tech-support and "federal agent" calls, gold-bar couriers, hacked exchanges. We read the court filings, collect the official statements and real FBI recordings, and turn each case into a documentary — with a short checklist you can send to your family.

How we work

  • Every case starts from a primary source: FBI and IC3 alerts, U.S. Department of Justice press releases, court filings, police statements.
  • Every fact on screen links to its source in the video description and on the case page.
  • We show a scam only as far as you need to recognise and stop it — never how to commit it.
  • Reconstructions are labelled RECONSTRUCTION. Narration uses AI voices; research, script and editing are assisted by Claude (Anthropic) and checked against the sources.
